Common Questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, and select Tradr to change or cancel.

Is my data private?
Yes. All your trade entries and notes are stored only on your device. Tradr does not collect or transmit your personal data. See our Privacy Policy.
