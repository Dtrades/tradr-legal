Privacy Policy for Tradr

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tradr ("the App") handles your information. The App is provided by Alicja Jankowska ("we," "us," or "our"). By using Tradr, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Tradr does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. The App does not require an account, login, or registration to use.

Data Stored on Your Device

All information you enter into Tradr — including trade entries, notes, and any images you attach — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. If you delete the App, this data is permanently removed from your device.

Photos and Images

Tradr may request access to your device's photo library so you can attach images to your trade entries. Any images you select are stored only on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or access these images.

Subscriptions and Payments

Tradr offers optional premium subscriptions purchased through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or have access to your payment details, credit card information, or billing data. Apple's handling of your payment information is governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

Tradr does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. The only third party involved is Apple, solely for processing optional subscription purchases.

Children's Privacy

Tradr does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no personal data, no information about any user is gathered or stored externally.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
